🚀 Quick Start — Do These First

A handful of one-time settings make every round after this one faster and more accurate. Do these in order the first time you open the app:

  • Have your Golf Canada username and password ready. You'll be asked once the first time you fetch any handicap, and the app remembers it after that — no need to log in again on this device.
  • Set yourself as "Me." In ⚙️ Manage My Players, find your name (or add yourself) and mark yourself as "Me." This auto-fills you as Player 1 every round and unlocks the personal settings below.
  • Set your Home City. Still in Manage My Players, on your "Me" entry. This drives the live weather shown on the Setup screen every time you open the app.
  • Set your Home Course. Also on your "Me" entry. Whatever course you pick always shows up first in the course picker and is selected automatically the moment you start a new round.
  • Set your preferred tee. Your Derrick tee deck (Deck 1 through Deck 4) is the reference point the app uses to automatically pick the closest-matching tee for you at every other course — no manual tee-hunting needed.
  • Add your regular players and their typical score. Save your regular group as contacts, star your most frequent playing partners as Favourites, and set each player's "Score Wheel Default" (Par, Bogey, or Double).
  • Fetch everyone's handicap. Once everyone has a Golf Canada ID on file, tap "🔄 Refresh All Handicaps" to pull every saved player's current Index in one batch.
Don't be surprised to see dozens of names already in Manage My Players the first time you open the app — a starter roster ships with the app and merges in safely by Golf Canada ID. It never overwrites or duplicates anyone you've already saved.

Privacy & Data Security

Everything stays on your device

All round data, player names, handicaps, betting results, and Golf Canada member IDs are stored exclusively in your iPhone's browser storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Golf Canada Member ID & Password

Your Golf Canada credentials are used only to fetch your current handicap index. They are stored only on your device, never transmitted to anyone other than Golf Canada's own secure API, and never included in exports or backups.

Pards Golf does not collect analytics, does not serve ads, has no user accounts, and has no server. Your data is yours alone.

1. Installing Pards Golf on Your iPhone

Pards Golf is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Add it to your Home Screen directly from Safari — not from the App Store.

You MUST install to your Home Screen before your data will be reliably saved. Opening in a browser tab means your data can be cleared at any time.
Do NOT use "Remove App" on the Home Screen icon — this permanently wipes all local data.

Step-by-Step Installation

1
Open Safari on your iPhone.
2
Go to: https://bdcomeau.github.io/pardsgolf/pards-golf.html
3
Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up, bottom toolbar).
4
Tap "Add to Home Screen."
5
Tap "Add" in the top-right corner.
6
Always launch from the Home Screen icon — not from a Safari bookmark.

Keeping the App Current

Tap Update App in the bottom action bar to check for updates. If still outdated, force-close and reopen, or clear Safari site data for bdcomeau.github.io (Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data).

Do NOT delete the Home Screen icon to update — this permanently erases all local data.

2. Setting Up a Round

Step 1 — Choose Your Course

Tap the course picker at the top. Your Home Course always appears first. Courses are organised into Canada and USA region accordions with provincial/state flag icons — tap a region to expand it. Tap 📋 Info on any course row to see live weather, your history there, and the full 18-hole scorecard.

Step 2 — Set Number of Players

Use + and − to set 1 to 5 players. The player tab bar updates live — one tab per player.

Step 3 — Load Your Favourites

Tap ⭐ Load My Favourites to fill in your saved regular group automatically.

Step 4 — Adding a Player

Tap the Select Saved Player button to open a searchable list of your saved contacts — sorted with "Me" first, then ⭐ Favourites, then alphabetically. Or type a new name directly:

  • Type their name, enter their handicap index, select their tee.
  • Optionally enter their 10-digit Golf Canada Member ID.
  • Tap 💾 Save to save as a contact for future rounds.

Step 5 — Fetch Handicaps from Golf Canada

If a Golf Canada ID is entered, tap the Fetch button on their row. First fetch asks for their password once — remembered for all future fetches.

Step 6 — Starting Hole

Defaults to Hole 1. Change for shotgun starts.

Step 7 — Scores Only or With Games?

  • With Games (default) — all betting games available.
  • Scores Only — no betting games.

Step 8 — Birdie Juice Tracking

Toggle 🍺 Birdie Juice Tracking on or off.

Step 9 — Configure Betting Games

Scroll to the BETTING GAMES section and use the game tab bar to configure each game. See Section 6 below.

Step 10 — Start the Round

Tap the gold Start Round → button.

Before any score is entered, the scorecard button says "✏️ Edit Round Settings" — tap it to go back to Setup.

3. Scoring — The Scorecard Screen

Entering Scores

Scroll each player's wheel to their score. A gold pending tile shows the score you've scrolled to — tap it to confirm, or tap the gold "✓ Confirm & Next Hole →" button at the bottom. A birdie or better triggers a celebration popup. Correct any score by scrolling again — everything recalculates instantly.

🆕 New in v19: Confirm Scores is now always ON. Auto-advance has been removed entirely. Every hole requires an explicit confirmation tap before advancing.

The Round Bar (During a Round)

During an active round, the full bottom navigation is replaced by a slim gold bar showing the current hole. This prevents accidental taps on History or Save & End Round while scoring.

  • Tap the bar itself to return to the scorecard from anywhere.
  • Tap ☰ Menu to open the round menu sheet.

The ☰ Menu (During a Round)

The round menu sheet slides up with all options:

  • Return to Scorecard — primary action, always at top
  • Games — access Nassau, 6-Point, Vegas and all betting games
  • Birdie Juice — view credits and tracker
  • Round History — view past rounds
  • Mid-Round Settings — change handicaps, tees, players, games
  • Save & End Round — complete and save the round

Mid-Round Settings

Access via ☰ Menu → Mid-Round Settings. All sections visible as collapsed headers — tap any to expand: Birdie Juice Tracking, Change Starting Hole, Players, 2v2 Nassau, 1v1 Nassau, 6-Point, Snake, Stableford, Skins, Vegas, Match Cards.

Any game can be added mid-round and will back-calculate results for holes already played.
Change the starting hole mid-round and the app shifts every score you've already entered to its correct new position — even Snake 3-putt history moves with it.

Completing a Round

On hole 18, once all scores are entered, the confirm button becomes "✓ Complete Round 🏆". Tapping it saves the round immediately and shows a gold pulsing banner with two options:

  • 👇 Start New Round — begin a fresh round
  • 📜 View & Share Results — navigate directly to the completed round in History

The scorecard stays visible so you can edit any hole if needed.

🆕 New in v20.29–30.10: Tapping 📜 View & Share Results now navigates directly to History and automatically opens your completed round — no need to find it in the list. The live round is fully retired so Match Cards and history editing work immediately.

5. Nassau Games

A three-part bet — Front 9, Back 9, Total 18 — each worth one bet unit. Auto-presses tracked automatically.

2v2 Nassau (High-Low)

Two teams of two. Each team contributes their Low ball and High ball net score. Up to three simultaneous 2v2 pairings with 4 players. A gold checkmark (✓) appears next to whichever player's score "counted" for their team on each hole — and carries through to every PDF, print, and shared export.

1v1 Nassau

One player vs another. Multiple matchups can run simultaneously.

Auto-Press

When a game reaches −2 margin, a press starts from the next hole. Presses can be pressed. All press history shown in the Games tab.

The International (The Big I)

Available after Hole 17 to the losing team. A single Hole 18 can cancel, double, or leave unchanged the entire pot.

  • Losing team wins Hole 18 → pot voided.
  • Leading team wins → pot doubles.
  • Halved → pot stays.

A full-screen reminder appears the first time you reach hole 18 with an eligible match. If you later edit any earlier score, the reminder automatically re-arms and prompts you again.

6. The 6-Point Game

Three-player game. Each hole: low net wins 4 pts, second wins 2, third wins 0. Points convert to dollars at round end. Ties for low give 3 pts each.

8. Skins

Each hole is its own contest. Win outright → win the skin. Tie → skin carries over. Select players, set dollars per skin, choose Gross or Net. After 18: Void (default) or Last Hole Sweeps.

Opens with no players selected — choose participants each round.

With five players at $5/skin, three carried skins on a hole is worth $15 to the winner.

9. Vegas

Two-against-two. Each team's scores combine low digit first (4 and 5 = 45). Teams play the difference per point. The Flip: an opponent birdie flips your number so the high digit leads (45 → 54). Requires 4 or 5 players. Multiple pairings can run simultaneously.

10. The Snake 3-Putt Money Game

Settles every 3 holes. Whoever holds the snake at holes 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18 pays each other participant the bet amount. Enable in the SNAKE tab, set bet per player, select participants.

11. Match Cards

Play 1v1 Nassau against golfers in OTHER groups. Your scores tracked live — opponents' scores entered later in the clubhouse. The ⚔️ Match Cards round lands in History with a purple border and 📝 Score Round pill when your round finishes.

After completing a round with Match Cards, tap 📜 View & Share Results to go straight to your round in History. From there you can tap the Match Cards entry separately to score your opponents.

13. The Course Picker

Courses are organised into Canada and USA region accordions with provincial/state flag icons. Your home course is always pinned at the top. Tap a region to expand it.

📋 Course Info

Tap 📋 Info on any course row: live weather, your history there (best, avg, lifetime money, last 12 scores vs par), and the full 18-hole scorecard with all tee yardages, ratings and slopes.

Weather Sources

Every course row shows its own current temperature, wind, and conditions. Edmonton-area courses share one efficient weather call; every other course fetches in small background batches so the list never feels slow to open.

  • Home City left blank or set to "Edmonton" — uses a personal WeatherLink station at Derrick.
  • Any other Home City — uses live Open-Meteo weather for that city.
🆕 New in v18–20: Canada/USA region accordions with flag icons. 140+ courses across 11 regions.

14. Round History

Access via ☰ Menu → Round History during a round, or the History tab on the setup screen.

  • Saving — tap 💾 Save Round, or rounds save automatically on New Round.
  • Locking — tap 🔓 to lock; hold 🔒 for 2 seconds to unlock.
  • Editing — tap ✏️ Edit on any completed round.
  • Pausing — tap ☰ Menu → End Round Early → 💾 Save & Pause Round. Resume from History.

15. Backup & Restore

Tap 💾 Backup / Data in the bottom action bar.

  • 📤 Export Backup — saves to Files, iCloud, or email.
  • 📥 Import Backup — restores from a previously exported file.
Export a backup regularly. iOS can wipe PWA storage when you remove the Home Screen icon, run low on storage, or during some iOS updates.

16. Starting a New Round

Tap 🔄 New Round in the bottom nav. Hold the confirmation button for 2 seconds. Rounds in progress show a red ⚠️ dialog — hold to abandon, release to cancel.

17. Manage My Players

Tap ⚙️ Manage My Players. A search bar floats at the bottom. Players are sorted with "Me" first, then ⭐ Favourites, then alphabetically.

  • 🔄 Refresh All Handicaps — fetches the latest Golf Canada Index for every saved player with a Golf Canada ID on file.
  • Mark yourself as "Me" — auto-fills Player 1 every round.
  • Star players to mark as Favourites.
  • Tee Deck — set preferred tee per course per player (remembered independently for every course).
  • Score Wheel Default — Par, Bogey, or Double as the starting point on the score entry wheel.
  • 🌤️ Home City (Me only) — sets which city's weather shows on the Setup screen.
  • ⛳ Home Course (Me only) — sets your default course. Always appears first in the course picker, selected automatically on every new round.
The tee you set in "Tee Deck" is remembered per course, per player — not just one global preference. Switch to a different course in the picker, open Manage My Players, and set that player's tee there; the app remembers that exact tee for that exact course forever.

19. Troubleshooting

My round disappeared 🏌️

Use 💾 Save & Pause Round before ending early. Without saving, data cannot be recovered.

The app looks outdated 🤔

Tap Update App. If that doesn't work, clear Safari site data for bdcomeau.github.io, force-close, reopen.

Pop-ups aren't appearing 😶

Run from the Home Screen icon, not a Safari tab.

Player data or history disappeared 😱

Likely caused by "Remove App." Restore from a backup file if available. Always export backups regularly.

A player left mid-round 🚑

Tap ☰ Menu → Mid-Round Settings and remove them there.

Weather not showing in course picker 🌦️

Weather loads in the background after launch. Wait a moment and reopen the picker.

Handicap strokes wrong ✏️

Formula: Index × (Slope ÷ 113) + (Course Rating − Par), rounded to nearest whole number. Check index, tee, and course are all correct.

History lock not working 🔒

Hold for the full 2 seconds without moving your finger — the icon fades as you hold.

Confirm button not appearing 🤔

Once all players have a score on the current hole, the gold "✓ Confirm & Next Hole →" button appears at the bottom of the scorecard.

"Active Round in Progress" shown when entering Match Cards 🚫

This should no longer occur after completing a round via 📜 View & Share Results (fixed in v30.10). If it appears unexpectedly, tap ☰ Menu → Save & End Round first, then try again.

A score or game looks wrong 🧮

  • Handicaps editable mid-round in ☰ Menu → Mid-Round Settings.
  • Scores correctable at any time — all totals recalculate instantly.
  • Wrong tee: go to Manage My Players → edit contact → set preferred tee.
