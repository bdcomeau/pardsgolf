🚀 Quick Start — Do These First

A handful of one-time settings make every round after this one faster and more accurate. Do these in order the first time you open the app:

  • Have your Golf Canada username and password ready. You'll be asked once the first time you fetch any handicap, and the app remembers it after that.
  • Set yourself as "Me." In ⚙️ Manage Players, find your name and mark yourself as "Me." This auto-fills you as Player 1 every round.
  • Set your Home City. On your "Me" entry. Drives live weather on the Setup screen.
  • Set your Home Course. On your "Me" entry. Appears first in the course picker and is auto-selected every new round.
  • Set your preferred tee. The app maps this to the closest-matching tee at every other course automatically.
  • Add your regular players. Save your group as contacts, star your frequent partners as Favourites, and set each player's Score Wheel Default.
  • Fetch everyone's handicap. Tap "🔄 Refresh All Handicaps" to pull all Golf Canada indexes at once.
Don't be surprised to see dozens of names already in Manage Players — a starter roster ships with the app and merges in safely by Golf Canada ID. It never overwrites anyone you've already saved.

Privacy & Data Security

Everything stays on your device

All round data, player names, handicaps, betting results, and Golf Canada member IDs are stored exclusively in your iPhone's browser storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device.

Golf Canada Member ID & Password

Your Golf Canada credentials are used only to fetch your current handicap index — stored only on your device, never transmitted to anyone other than Golf Canada's own secure API.

Pards Golf does not collect analytics, does not serve ads, has no user accounts, and has no server. Your data is yours alone.

1. Installing Pards Golf on Your iPhone

Pards Golf is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Add it to your Home Screen directly from Safari — not from the App Store.

You MUST install to your Home Screen before your data will be reliably saved.
Do NOT use "Remove App" on the Home Screen icon — this permanently wipes all local data.

Step-by-Step Installation

1
Open Safari on your iPhone.
2
Go to: https://bdcomeau.github.io/pardsgolf/pards-golf.html
3
Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up, bottom toolbar).
4
Tap "Add to Home Screen."
5
Tap "Add" in the top-right corner.
6
Always launch from the Home Screen icon — not from a Safari bookmark.

Keeping the App Current

Tap Update App in the bottom action bar to check for updates. If still outdated, clear Safari site data for bdcomeau.github.io, force-close, reopen.

2. Setting Up a Round

🆕 New in v40.0: The Setup screen has been redesigned to fit everything — including 5 players — on a single screen without scrolling.

Step 1 — Choose Your Course

Tap the course picker at the top. Your Home Course always appears first. Courses are organised into Canada and USA region accordions — tap a region to expand it. Tap 📋 Info on any course row to see live weather, your history, and the full scorecard.

Step 2 — Configure the Strip

Three compact pills replace the old setup cards:

👥
Players
Tap to set 1–5 players with a +/− counter
🚩
Starts
Tap to pick starting hole (1–18 grid)
⚙️
Options
Games, Birdie Juice, and Reset

The ⚙️ Options sheet contains:

  • 📋 Scorekeeping Mode Only — ON removes all betting games; OFF (default) enables full game tracking
  • 🍺 Birdie Juice Tracking — toggle on or off
  • 🧹 Reset Round Setup — hold for 2 seconds to clear all players and games. This returns to the default: 4 players, Me in slot 1, 3 empty slots

Step 3 — Load Players

Two equal buttons appear below the PLAYERS divider:

  • ⭐ Load My Favourites — fills all player slots from your starred contacts instantly
  • ⚙️ Manage Players — opens the full player management screen to add, edit, or configure players

Step 4 — Adjust Individual Players

Each player row shows their name, tee deck, adjusted HCP, and a ↻ refresh button:

  • Tap the player name to open a picker — Me first, then Favourites, then alphabetical. Tap ＋ New Player at the top to create a new player in Manage Players.
  • Selecting a player automatically loads their saved preferred tee for the active course and recalculates their adjusted HCP.
  • Tap the tee button to change tees for this round. The HCP recalculates immediately.
  • Tap ↻ (green = Golf Canada ID on file) to fetch a fresh handicap from Golf Canada and recalculate immediately.
Every fresh round starts with 4 players, Me in slot 1, and 3 empty slots ready to fill.

Step 5 — Start the Round

Tap the gold ⛳ Start Round → button. The Round Launch Summary appears for final review before play begins.

Before any score is entered, tap "✏️ Edit Round Settings" to return to Setup.

3. Scoring — The Scorecard Screen

Entering Scores

Scroll each player's wheel to their score. A gold pending tile shows the score you've scrolled to — tap it to confirm, or tap the gold "✓ Confirm & Next Hole →" button at the bottom. A birdie or better triggers a celebration popup. Correct any score by scrolling again — everything recalculates instantly.

Confirm Scores is always ON. Every hole requires an explicit confirmation tap before advancing. There is no auto-advance mode.

The Round Bar (During a Round)

During an active round, the full bottom navigation is replaced by a slim gold bar showing the current hole.

  • Tap the bar to return to the scorecard from anywhere.
  • Tap ☰ Menu to open the round menu sheet.

The ☰ Menu (During a Round)

  • Return to Scorecard
  • Games — access all betting games
  • Birdie Juice — view credits and tracker
  • Round History — view past rounds
  • Mid-Round Settings — change handicaps, tees, players, games
  • Save & End Round

Mid-Round Settings

Access via ☰ Menu → Mid-Round Settings. All sections visible as collapsed headers — tap any to expand.

Any game can be added mid-round and will back-calculate results for holes already played.

Completing a Round

On hole 18, once all scores are entered, the confirm button becomes "✓ Complete Round 🏆". Tapping it saves the round immediately and offers:

  • 👇 Start New Round
  • 📜 View & Share Results — navigates directly to the completed round in History

5. Nassau Games

A three-part bet — Front 9, Back 9, Total 18. Auto-presses tracked automatically.

2v2 Nassau (High-Low)

Two teams of two. Each team contributes their Low ball and High ball net score. A gold ✓ appears next to whichever player's score counted.

1v1 Nassau

One player vs another. Multiple matchups can run simultaneously.

The International (The Big I)

Available after Hole 17 to the losing team. A single Hole 18 can cancel, double, or leave unchanged the entire pot. A full-screen reminder appears at hole 18.

6. The 6-Point Game

Three-player game. Each hole: low net wins 4 pts, second wins 2, third wins 0. Ties for low give 3 pts each.

8. Skins

Win outright → win the skin. Tie → skin carries over. Select players, set dollars per skin, choose Gross or Net. After 18: Void (default) or Last Hole Sweeps.

9. Vegas

Two-against-two. Scores combine low digit first (4 and 5 = 45). The Flip: an opponent birdie flips your number (45 → 54).

10. The Snake 3-Putt Money Game

Settles every 3 holes. Whoever holds the snake at holes 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18 pays each other participant the bet amount.

11. Match Cards

Play 1v1 Nassau against up to 4 golfers in OTHER groups. Your scores tracked live — opponents' scores entered later in the clubhouse via History.

After completing a round with Match Cards, tap 📜 View & Share Results to go straight to your round in History and score your opponents.

13. The Course Picker

Courses are organised into Canada and USA region accordions. Your home course is always pinned at the top. Tap 📋 Info on any course row to see live weather, your history, and the full 18-hole scorecard.

14. Round History

Access via ☰ Menu → Round History during a round, or the History tab on the setup screen.

  • Saving — tap 💾 Save Round, or rounds save automatically on New Round.
  • Locking — tap 🔓 to lock; hold 🔒 for 2 seconds to unlock.
  • Editing — tap ✏️ Edit on any completed round.
  • Pausing — tap ☰ Menu → End Round Early → 💾 Save & Pause Round. Resume from History.

15. Backup & Restore

Tap 💾 Backup / Data in the bottom action bar.

  • 📤 Export Backup — saves to Files, iCloud, or email.
  • 📥 Import Backup — restores from a previously exported file.
Export a backup regularly. iOS can wipe PWA storage when you remove the Home Screen icon or run low on storage.

16. Starting a New Round

Tap 🔄 New Round in the bottom nav. Hold the confirmation button for 2 seconds. The setup screen resets to 4 players with Me in slot 1.

17. Manage Players

🆕 Completely rebuilt in v40.0 — full player profiles with career stats, a clean two-panel interface, and custom pickers for all settings.

Tap ⚙️ Manage Players from the Setup screen or the bottom navigation.

Player List

  • Shows all saved players: Me first → ⭐ Favourites → alphabetical
  • Tap any row to open their full profile and edit screen
  • Tap the ★/☆ star to instantly favourite or unfavourite — no need to open the record
  • Tap ＋ Add New Player to create a new player
  • Tap 🔄 Refresh All Handicaps to fetch Golf Canada indexes for everyone at once

A hint at the top of the list reads: Tap any player to view or edit · Tap ★ to favourite

Player Profile / Edit Screen

Tap any player row to open their full profile. Tap ‹ Players (or anywhere on the header bar) to return to the list. If you've made changes, tapping back shows an action sheet: Save & Return / Discard & Return / Cancel.

Toggles

  • ★ Favourite — tap to toggle on/off instantly. Saves immediately — no Save Changes needed. Me is always a favourite and cannot be unstarred. Maximum 4 non-Me favourites.
  • ✓ This is Me — tap to see an immediate warning dialog explaining the impact. Inside the dialog, hold Hold to Confirm for 2 seconds to enact the change. This prevents accidental Me changes which affect career earnings attribution.

Identity

  • Player Name — renaming a player without a GC ID who has history shows a warning about data integrity.
  • Golf Canada ID — the field is locked with 🔒 once a GC ID is saved. Hold 🔒 for 2 seconds to unlock and edit. This protects against accidental changes that would break handicap fetching.
  • Fetch HCP button — activates the moment you type a valid 10-digit GC ID. Fetches live from Golf Canada and updates the index and adjusted HCP immediately.

Handicap & Tee

  • Golf Canada Index — auto-populated by Fetch; can also be entered manually for players without a GC ID.
  • Adjusted HCP — automatically calculated from Index × Slope formula for the selected tee. Updates live as you change index or tee.
  • Preferred Tee — opens a custom dark green picker showing all tee boxes with coloured badges and yardage. The selected tee is remembered per player per course.
  • Score Wheel Default — Par, Bogey, or Double. Sets the starting position of the score entry wheel for this player.

Me Settings (Me players only)

  • Home City — sets which city's weather shows on the Setup screen.
  • Home Course — sets your default course. Changing this automatically updates the Preferred Tee picker to show that course's tee boxes.

Career Stats (read-only)

  • Career Birdie Juice — total gross birdies automatically counted from all history rounds.
  • Overall Match Record — W/L/T across all games (accumulates going forward from v40.0).
  • vs [Me Name] — head-to-head record against the Me player.
  • Career Earnings — cumulative net money across all recorded rounds.
  • Total Rounds Played / Last Round Played — non-Me players only.

Favourite Rules

  • Me is always a favourite and cannot be unstarred
  • Maximum 4 other players can be starred (5 total including Me)
  • ⭐ Load My Favourites fills all player slots from your starred players
The tee you set in Preferred Tee is remembered per player per course — not just one global preference. Switch to a different course and each player's tee preference for that specific course is recalled automatically.

19. Troubleshooting

My round disappeared 🏌️

Use 💾 Save & Pause Round before ending early. Without saving, data cannot be recovered.

The app looks outdated 🤔

Tap Update App. If that doesn't work, clear Safari site data for bdcomeau.github.io, force-close, reopen.

Pop-ups aren't appearing 😶

Run from the Home Screen icon, not a Safari tab.

Player data or history disappeared 😱

Likely caused by "Remove App." Restore from a backup file if available. Always export backups regularly.

A player left mid-round 🚑

Tap ☰ Menu → Mid-Round Settings and remove them there.

Weather not showing in course picker 🌦️

Weather loads in the background after launch. Wait a moment and reopen the picker.

Handicap strokes wrong ✏️

Formula: Index × (Slope ÷ 113) + (Course Rating − Par), rounded to nearest whole number. Check index, tee, and course are all correct.

History lock not working 🔒

Hold for the full 2 seconds without moving your finger.

Confirm button not appearing 🤔

Once all players have a score on the current hole, the gold "✓ Confirm & Next Hole →" button appears at the bottom of the scorecard. Confirm Scores is always ON.

Save Changes in Manage Players does nothing 🔧

The Save Changes button only appears after you've actually changed a field. If no fields have changed from their original values, the button stays hidden — this is by design.

A score or game looks wrong 🧮

  • Handicaps are editable mid-round in ☰ Menu → Mid-Round Settings.
  • Scores are correctable at any time — all totals recalculate instantly.
  • Wrong tee: go to Manage Players → open the player → change Preferred Tee.
