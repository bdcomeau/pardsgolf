🚀 Quick Start — Do These First

A handful of one-time settings make every round faster and more accurate. Do these in order the first time you open the app:

  • Have your Golf Canada username and password ready. You'll be asked once the first time you fetch any handicap — the app remembers after that.
  • Set yourself as "Me." In ⚙️ Manage Players, find your name and mark yourself as "Me." This auto-fills you as Player 1 every round.
  • Set your Home City on your "Me" entry. Drives live weather on the Setup screen.
  • Set your Home Course on your "Me" entry. Appears first in the course picker and is auto-selected every new round.
  • Set your preferred tee. The app maps this to the closest-matching tee at every other course automatically.
  • Add your regular players. Save your group, star your frequent partners as Favourites, and set each player's Score Wheel Default.
  • Fetch everyone's handicap. Tap "🔄 Refresh All Handicaps" to pull all Golf Canada indexes at once.
Don't be surprised to see dozens of names already in Manage Players — a starter roster ships with the app and merges safely by Golf Canada ID. It never overwrites anyone you've already saved.

Privacy & Data Security

Everything stays on your device

All round data, player names, handicaps, betting results, and Golf Canada member IDs are stored exclusively in your iPhone's browser storage. This data never leaves your device.

Golf Canada Member ID & Password

Your credentials are used only to fetch your current handicap index — stored only on your device, never transmitted to anyone other than Golf Canada's own secure API.

Pards Golf does not collect analytics, does not serve ads, has no user accounts, and has no server. Your data is yours alone.

1. Installing Pards Golf on Your iPhone

Pards Golf is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Add it to your Home Screen directly from Safari — not from the App Store.

You MUST install to your Home Screen before your data will be reliably saved.
Do NOT use "Remove App" on the Home Screen icon — this permanently wipes all local data.

Step-by-Step Installation

1
Open Safari on your iPhone.
2
Go to: https://bdcomeau.github.io/pardsgolf/pards-golf.html
3
Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up, bottom toolbar).
4
Tap "Add to Home Screen."
5
Tap "Add" in the top-right corner.
6
Always launch from the Home Screen icon — not from a Safari bookmark.

Keeping the App Current

Tap Update App in the bottom action bar to check for updates. If still outdated, clear Safari site data for bdcomeau.github.io, force-close, reopen.

2. Setting Up a Round

Step 1 — Choose Your Course

Tap the course picker at the top. Your Home Course always appears first. Courses are organised into Canada and USA region accordions — tap a region to expand it. Tap 📋 Info on any course row to see live weather, your history, and the full scorecard.

Step 2 — Configure the Strip

Two compact pills handle player count and starting hole:

👥
Players
Tap to set 1–5 players with +/− buttons
🚩
Starts
Tap to pick starting hole (1–18 grid)

Step 3 — Load Players

  • ⭐ Load My Favourites — fills all player slots from your starred contacts instantly.
  • ⚙️ Manage Players — opens the full player management screen.
  • Tap any player row to open a picker (Me → Favourites → alphabetical). Tap ＋ New Player to create someone new.
  • Selecting a player automatically loads their saved preferred tee for the active course and recalculates adjusted HCP.
  • Tap the tee button to change tees for this round.
  • Tap ↻ (green = Golf Canada ID on file) to fetch a fresh handicap.
Every fresh round starts with 4 players, Me in slot 1, and 3 empty slots ready to fill.

Step 4 — Settings

Tap ⚙️ Settings in the bottom action bar to open the Settings & Help overlay:

  • 🎮 Games — ON (default) enables all betting games. OFF switches to scorekeeping-only mode and hides all game config.
  • 🐦🥃 Birdie Juice — toggle tracking on or off.
  • 🧹 Start Over — hold for 2 seconds to clear all players and games. Returns to: 4 players, Me in slot 1, home course, hole 1.

Step 5 — Configure Betting Games

Eight game pills appear below the player section. Tap any pill to open that game's config overlay. A green dot on a pill means that game is configured and active. See Section 6: Configuring Betting Games for details.

Step 6 — Start

Tap the gold ⛳ Start Round → button. The Round Launch Summary appears for final review.

Before any score is entered, tap "✏️ Edit Round Settings" to return to Setup.

3. Scoring — The Scorecard Screen

Entering Scores

Scroll each player's wheel to their score. A gold pending tile shows the score you've scrolled to — tap it to confirm, or tap the gold "✓ Confirm & Next Hole →" button at the bottom. A birdie or better triggers a celebration popup. Correct any score by scrolling again — everything recalculates instantly.

Confirm Scores is always ON. Every hole requires an explicit confirmation tap before advancing.

The Scorecard Action Bar

A slim fixed row at the top of the round bar gives quick access to:

  • 💵 Game Cards — view live Nassau, Vegas, and all game results
  • 🐦🥃 Birdie Juice — view credits and tracker (red badge shows remaining credits)
  • ⚙️ Settings — mid-round settings: handicaps, tees, players, games
  • 🏁 End Round — save, pause, or abandon the round

Mid-Round Settings

Tap ⚙️ Settings during a round. All sections visible as collapsed headers — tap any to expand. Any game can be added mid-round and will back-calculate results for holes already played.

Completing a Round

On hole 18, once all scores are entered, the confirm button becomes "✓ Complete Round 🏆". Tapping it saves the round and offers:

  • 👇 Start New Round
  • 📜 View & Share Results — goes straight to your completed round in History

Ball Toss (Nassau 2v2 & Vegas)

Both 2v2 games use a ball toss to pick teams. Tap 2 partners in the grid — matches generate automatically:

  • 4 players: 1 match (toss partners vs the other two)
  • 5 players: 3 matches — the toss partners become the Swing Team, playing one match against each combo of the other three

Each match card shows its own bet amount and ON/OFF toggle. Tap ⚙️ Manual team selection to pick matchups yourself instead.

Inline Config Pattern

For 1v1 Nassau, 6-Point, Stableford, Skins, Vegas (manual), and Match Cards — tap any row or player to activate it. The row expands inline with bet buttons and a ✕ Remove option. No separate active list appears below.

5. Nassau Games

A three-part bet — Front 9, Back 9, Total 18. Auto-presses tracked automatically.

2v2 Nassau (High-Low)

Two teams of two. Each team contributes their Low ball and High ball net score per hole. Use the ball toss to pick teams — tap 2 partners and matches are generated automatically. A gold ✓ appears next to whichever player's score counted on each hole.

1v1 Nassau

One player vs another. Multiple matchups can run simultaneously. Tap a matchup row to activate it — bet buttons and Remove appear inline. Each matchup can have its own bet amount.

The International (The Big I)

Available after Hole 17 to the losing team. A single Hole 18 can cancel, double, or leave unchanged the entire pot. A full-screen reminder appears at hole 18 so you never forget to offer it.

6. The 6-Point Game

Three-player game. Each hole: low net wins 4 pts, second wins 2, third wins 0. Ties for low give 3 pts each. 6 points in play on every hole — highest total over 18 wins.

Tap any 3-player combo to activate it. Bet options run from 25¢ to $10 per point. Multiple groups of 3 can run simultaneously from the same pool of players.

7. Stableford

Points-based individual game — full handicap applied. Highest points total wins; losers pay the winner the points difference × bet per point.

Tap player chips to include them. Once selected, a points table appears with +/− controls to adjust any score category, plus a bet per point row. Tap ↺ Reset to restore default point values.

8. Skins

Win a hole outright to claim the skin. Ties carry the skin over to the next hole — pots build up. Tap player chips to include them, then configure:

  • Value per skin — $1, $2, $5, $10, or $20
  • Scoring — Net (handicap applied) or Gross
  • On a tie — Carry over (skin accumulates) or No carry
  • After hole 18 (if carry is on) — Wash (leftovers void) or Last Hole Sweeps (hole 18 takes the accumulated pot)

9. Vegas

Two-against-two. Each team combines their two scores into a two-digit number, lowest digit first — a 4 and a 5 become 45. Teams play the difference × bet each hole. Requires 4 or 5 players.

The Flip: if the opposing team makes a birdie, your number flips high-digit first (45 becomes 54). High variance, high drama.

Use the ball toss to pick teams (same as Nassau 2v2 — tap 2 partners, matches auto-generate with Swing Team for 5-player groups). Each match card lets you set $/point, Gross/Net, and Flip on/off independently. Tap ⚙️ Manual team selection if you'd rather pick matchups yourself.

10. The Snake 3-Putt Money Game

Any 3-putt picks up the snake 🐍. Whoever holds the snake at settlement holes (3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18) pays every other participant the bet amount. The snake resets after each settlement.

Configure participants and the bet per settlement in the Snake overlay. The snake holder is tracked live on the scorecard.

11. Match Cards

Play 1v1 Nassau against up to 4 golfers in other groups. Your scores are tracked live during your round. Opponents' scores are entered later in the clubhouse from History — your scores are pre-filled automatically.

Tap ⚔️ Enable Match Cards to turn it on, then use the 4 opponent tabs to add each opponent. For each opponent you can:

  • Select from your saved players or type a name manually
  • Set their handicap index and preferred tee (adjusted HCP calculates automatically)
  • Fetch their Golf Canada handicap live if they have a GC ID on file
  • Set the Nassau bet per side
After completing a round with Match Cards, tap 📜 View & Share Results to go straight to your round in History and enter opponents' scores.

13. The Course Picker

Tap the course picker at the top of Setup. Your home course is always pinned first. Courses are organised into Canada and USA region accordions with flag icons. Tap 📋 Info on any row for live weather, your round history at that course, and the full 18-hole scorecard with all tee yardages, ratings, and slopes.

14. Round History

Access via the History tab on the Setup screen, or via ⚙️ Settings → Round History during a round.

  • Saving — rounds save automatically when you start a new round. Tap 💾 Save Round to save manually at any time.
  • Locking — tap 🔓 to lock a round; hold 🔒 for 2 seconds to unlock.
  • Editing — tap ✏️ Edit on any completed round to change scores or game results.
  • Pausing — tap 🏁 End Round → Save & Pause Round to pause mid-round and resume later from History.

15. Backup & Restore

Tap 💾 Backup / Data in the bottom action bar.

  • 📤 Export Backup — saves to Files, iCloud, or email.
  • 📥 Import Backup — restores from a previously exported file.
Export a backup regularly. iOS can wipe PWA storage when you remove the Home Screen icon or run low on storage.

16. Manage Players

Tap ⚙️ Manage Players from the Setup screen or the bottom navigation.

Player List

  • Shows all saved players: Me first → ⭐ Favourites → alphabetical
  • Tap any row to open their full profile
  • Tap the ★/☆ star to instantly favourite or unfavourite — no need to open the record
  • Tap ＋ Add New Player to create a new player
  • Tap 🔄 Refresh All Handicaps to fetch Golf Canada indexes for everyone at once

Player Profile / Edit Screen

Tap any player row to open their full profile. Tap ‹ Players to return. If you've made changes, tapping back shows an action sheet: Save & Return / Discard & Return / Cancel.

Toggles

  • ★ Favourite — tap to toggle instantly. Me is always a favourite. Maximum 4 non-Me favourites (5 total).
  • ✓ This is Me — tap to see a warning. Hold Hold to Confirm for 2 seconds to change the Me player.

Identity

  • Player Name — renaming a player without a GC ID who has history shows a data integrity warning.
  • Golf Canada ID — locked with 🔒 once set. Hold 🔒 for 2 seconds to unlock.
  • Fetch HCP — activates the moment you type a valid 10-digit GC ID.

Handicap & Tee

  • Golf Canada Index — auto-populated by Fetch; can be entered manually.
  • Adjusted HCP — calculated from Index × (Slope ÷ 113) + (Course Rating − Par). Updates live.
  • Preferred Tee — remembered per player per course. Custom picker with coloured badges and yardage.
  • Score Wheel Default — Par, Bogey, or Double. Sets the score entry wheel starting position.

Me Settings (Me players only)

  • Home City — drives weather on the Setup screen.
  • Home Course — auto-selected on new rounds. Changing it updates the Preferred Tee picker.

Career Stats (read-only)

  • Career Birdie Juice — total gross birdies scanned from all history rounds.
  • Overall Match Record — W/L/T across all games.
  • vs [Me Name] — head-to-head record against the Me player.
  • Career Earnings — cumulative net money across all rounds.
  • Total Rounds Played / Last Round Played — non-Me players only.
Preferred tee is remembered per player per course — not a single global preference. Switch to a different course and each player's tee for that course is recalled automatically.

18. Troubleshooting

My round disappeared 🏌️

Use 🏁 End Round → Save & Pause Round before ending early. Without saving, data cannot be recovered.

The app looks outdated 🤔

Tap Update App. If that doesn't work, clear Safari site data for bdcomeau.github.io, force-close, reopen.

Pop-ups aren't appearing 😶

Run from the Home Screen icon, not a Safari tab.

Player data or history disappeared 😱

Likely caused by "Remove App." Restore from a backup file if available. Always export backups regularly.

A player left mid-round 🚑

Tap ⚙️ Settings during the round → Players section → remove them there.

Weather not showing in course picker 🌦️

Weather loads in the background after launch. Wait a moment and reopen the picker.

Handicap strokes wrong ✏️

Formula: Index × (Slope ÷ 113) + (Course Rating − Par), rounded to nearest whole number. Check index, tee, and course are all correct.

History lock not working 🔒

Hold for the full 2 seconds without moving your finger.

Confirm button not appearing 🤔

Once all players have a score on the current hole, the gold "✓ Confirm & Next Hole →" button appears at the bottom of the scorecard. Confirm Scores is always ON.

Nassau 2v2 pill shows green dot but no games set 🟢

This was fixed in v50.0. If you still see it after updating, tap the 🏆 Nassau 2v2 pill, verify no matches are listed, and close the overlay — the dot will clear.

A score or game looks wrong 🧮

  • Handicaps are editable mid-round via ⚙️ Settings.
  • Scores are correctable at any time — all totals recalculate instantly.
  • Wrong tee: go to Manage Players → open the player → change Preferred Tee.
