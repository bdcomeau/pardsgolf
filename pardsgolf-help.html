Installing Pards Golf

Pards Golf is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Add it to your iPhone Home Screen directly from Safari — not the App Store.

⚠️ Important: You MUST install to your Home Screen before your data will be reliably saved.

⚠️ Never use "Remove App" on the Home Screen icon — this permanently wipes all local data. Export a backup first.
  1. 1
    Open Safari on your iPhone (not Chrome or Firefox)
  2. 2
    Go to: https://bdcomeau.github.io/pardsgolf/pards-golf.html
  3. 3
    Tap the Share button (square with arrow, bottom toolbar)
  4. 4
    Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  5. 5
    Tap "Add" in the top-right corner
  6. 6
    Always launch from the Home Screen icon — not a Safari bookmark

Keeping the App Current

Tap Update App in the bottom utility bar on the setup screen. If still outdated, force-close Safari and reopen, or clear Safari site data for bdcomeau.github.io.

Quick Start

A few one-time settings make every round faster and more accurate:

  • Set yourself as "Me." In ⚙️ Manage My Players, find your name and mark yourself as "Me." This auto-fills you as Player 1 every round.
  • Set your Home Course. Appears first in the course picker, auto-selected every new round.
  • Set your preferred tee. Maps to the closest-matching tee at every other course.
  • Add regular players. Star as Favourites, set Score Wheel Defaults (Par, Bogey, or Double).
  • Fetch handicaps. Tap "🔄 Refresh All Handicaps" to pull all Golf Canada indexes at once.
  • Set your Home City. Drives live weather on the Setup screen.
💡 Tip: Don't be surprised to see dozens of names already in Manage My Players — a starter roster ships with the app and merges safely by Golf Canada ID. It never overwrites anyone you've already saved.

Step 1 — Choose Your Course

Tap the course picker at the top. Your Home Course always appears first. Courses are organised into Canada and USA region accordions. Tap 📋 Info on any row to see live weather, your round history, and the full scorecard with all tee yardages, ratings and slopes.

Step 2 — Set Player Count

Tap + and − on the Players pill to set 1–5 players. Tap ⭐ Load My Favourites to fill in your regular group automatically.

Step 3 — Enter Players

Tap any player row to open the player picker — a full-screen sheet with Me, Buddies, and All Players sections (all expanded). Live search filters instantly. To remove a player, tap their row and tap ✕ Remove Player at the top of the sheet (shows the player's name so you know who you're removing).

💡 Quick Add players are automatically Buddies. To change their Buddy status, remove them from Quick Add first.

Step 4 — Starting Hole

Default is Hole 1. Change for shotgun starts. If you score the wrong hole, use Mid-Round Settings → Change Starting Hole to shift all scores to the correct holes.

Step 5 — Choose Games

Tap any of the 8 illustrated game pills to configure that game. Active games show a gold dot. Tap again to open the game's setup overlay. Games not applicable to your player count are automatically hidden.

🆕 v70.4: Game pill text labels removed — the illustrated icons make each game self-evident. Icons enlarged to 64px.

Step 6 — Start Round

Tap the gold ⛳ Start Round → button. Validation runs immediately — missing handicaps, duplicate names, or no games selected will show an error. Once valid, you go straight to the scorecard.

The Setup Utility Bar

Four buttons pinned to the bottom of the setup screen:

  • ⚙️ Settings / Help — opens the user manual
  • ☁️ Update App — checks for updates
  • 📋 Round History — view past rounds
  • 💾 Backup — export/import all data

Entering Scores

Scroll each player's wheel to their score. A gold highlighted cell shows the score selected. The label beneath each score shows its name (Birdie, Par, Bogey, etc.). Tap the gold ✓ Confirm & Next Hole → button to advance. Every hole requires an explicit confirmation tap.

Correct any score by scrolling again at any point — everything recalculates instantly. A birdie or better triggers a celebration popup.

🆕 v70.4: Auto-advance has been permanently removed. Confirm Scores is always ON — tap the gold button to advance every hole.

Score Wheel Defaults

Each player's wheel centers on their Score Wheel Default — Par, Bogey, or Double — set in Manage My Players. This means the wheel starts right where they typically live, reducing scrolling.

Completing a Round

On hole 18, once all scores are entered, the confirm button becomes ✓ Complete Round 🏆. Tapping it saves the round immediately to History and shows a gold banner. Two options appear: Start New Round and View & Share Results. The scorecard stays visible — edit any hole if needed.

The Score Screen Bottom Bar

Four quick-access buttons at the bottom during scoring:

  • Game Cards — view Nassau, 6-Point, and all bet totals
  • Birdie Juice — view credits and tracker
  • Settings — opens Mid-Round Settings
  • End Round — opens the round menu

Skins

Win outright → win the skin. Tie → skin carries over to the next hole (carryover). Choose Gross or Net scoring.

After Hole 18:

  • Void (default) — any unresolved carried skins disappear
  • Last Hole Sweeps — the winner of hole 18 takes all remaining carryover skins

Vegas

Two-against-two. Each team's two scores combine low digit first to form a two-digit number. Teams play the difference.

Player ScoresTeam Number
4 and 545
3 and 636
5 and 555

The Flip

If an opponent makes a birdie, your team's number gets flipped (45 → 54). This is the key strategic element of Vegas.

💡 Vegas works with 5 players — pick any two teams of two, and the 5th player sits out the Vegas game.

Snake 3-Putt

Whoever 3-putts picks up the snake 🐍. The snake passes to whoever 3-putts next. It settles every 3 holes.

Settlement holes: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18. Whoever holds the snake at a settlement hole pays each other participant.

Match Cards

Play 1v1 Nassau vs up to 4 players in other groups. Your scores are tracked live — opponents' scores are entered later from Round History in the clubhouse.

  1. 1
    Enable Match Cards on the setup screen and add up to 4 opponents by name
  2. 2
    Play your round normally — your scores are tracked in real time
  3. 3
    After the round, go to Round History → your Match Cards entry → enter opponents' scores
  4. 4
    Nassau results calculate automatically once all scores are in
💡 Opponent picker: Tap the player select area for a full-screen contact picker with live search. Opponents don't need to be saved contacts — type a name and index directly.

Backup & Restore

⚠️ Export a backup regularly. iOS can wipe PWA storage when you remove the Home Screen icon or run low on storage. Once data is gone without a backup, it cannot be recovered.

What's Included in a Backup

  • ✅ All player contacts
  • ✅ All round history
  • ✅ Current round in progress
  • ✅ Side bets data
  • ✅ Golf Canada credentials
  • ✅ Scorecard-only preference
  • ✅ Setup screen state

How to Export a Backup

  1. 1
    Tap Backup in the setup utility bar
  2. 2
    Tap 📤 Export Backup
  3. 3
    Your iPhone share sheet opens — save to Files, iCloud Drive, or email it to yourself

How to Restore

  1. 1
    Tap Backup in the setup utility bar
  2. 2
    Tap 📥 Import Backup
  3. 3
    Select your backup JSON file
  4. 4
    The app reloads with all restored data
🆕 v70.4: Backup now includes Side Bets data, Golf Canada credentials, Scorecard-only preference, and Setup screen state — previously these were not backed up.

Manage My Players

Tap ⚙️ Settings on the setup screen → Manage My Players. Players are sorted: "Me" first → ⭐ Favourites → alphabetically.

Per-Player Settings

  • Me — marks you as Player 1 auto-fill
  • ⭐ Favourite / Buddy — appears in the Buddies section of the player picker
  • ⚡ Quick Add — loads automatically with Load My Favourites. Quick Add players are always Buddies.
  • Golf Canada ID — 10-digit ID for handicap fetching
  • Score Wheel Default — Par, Bogey, or Double — pre-positions the score wheel
  • Preferred Tee/Deck — maps to closest tee at every course
  • 🌤️ Home City — drives Setup screen weather (Me only)
  • ⛳ Home Course — auto-selected every new round (Me only)

Bulk Handicap Refresh

Tap 🔄 Refresh All Handicaps to pull the latest Golf Canada Index for every player with a Golf Canada ID entered.

Privacy & Data Security

Everything Stays on Your Device

All round data, player names, handicaps, betting results, and Golf Canada member IDs are stored exclusively in your iPhone's browser storage. This data never leaves your device.

Golf Canada Credentials

Your credentials are used only to fetch your handicap index — stored only on your device, never transmitted to anyone other than Golf Canada's own secure API.

📌 Pards Golf does not collect analytics, does not serve ads, has no user accounts, and has no server. Your data is yours alone.

Troubleshooting

My round disappeared 🏌️

Use 💾 Save & Pause Round before ending early. Without saving, data cannot be recovered.

The app looks outdated 🤔

Tap Update App. If that doesn't work, clear Safari site data for bdcomeau.github.io, force-close, and reopen.

Pop-ups aren't appearing 😶

Run from the Home Screen icon, not a Safari tab.

Player data or history disappeared 😱

Likely caused by "Remove App." Restore from a backup file if available.

A player left mid-round 🚑

Tap Mid-Round Settings → Players & Handicaps. Tap their player row and select Remove Player at the top of the picker.

Weather not showing 🌦️

Weather loads in the background after launch. Wait a moment and reopen the course picker.

Handicap strokes wrong ✏️

Formula: Index × (Slope ÷ 113) + (Course Rating − Par), rounded to nearest whole number. Check that the correct index, tee, and course are all set.

History lock not working 🔒

Hold for the full 2 seconds without moving your finger — the icon fades as you hold.

Confirm button not appearing 🤔

Confirm Scores is always ON. Once all players have a score entered on the current hole, the gold ✓ Confirm button appears at the bottom of the scorecard.

Start Round button not responding 🚫

Scroll down — the button may be partially behind the utility bar on some iPhone sizes. The app now has increased padding to prevent this.
